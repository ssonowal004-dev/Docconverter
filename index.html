🔒 The Short Version

  • Your documents and images are never uploaded to any server. Everything runs in your browser.
  • We collect basic, anonymous analytics (page views). No personal data is tied to this.
  • We may show ads, which have their own cookies from ad networks.
  • We do not sell your data to anyone, ever.
  • If you email us, we keep that email to respond to you and nothing more.

1 Overview

DocConverter ("we", "us", "our") operates the website at docconverter.app. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information in connection with your use of our Service.

DocConverter is built on a privacy-by-design architecture. The core functionality — document and image processing — requires no personal data and involves no server-side processing of your files.

🔒 Our architecture makes it technically impossible for us to view, access, or store the files you process. This is not just a policy — it is how the code works.

2 Your Files — The Most Important Part

When you use any DocConverter tool (PDF to Word, Compress PDF, Merge PDF, etc.), your files are processed entirely within your web browser using JavaScript libraries (pdf-lib, PDF.js, mammoth.js, docx.js, browser-image-compression).

This means:

  • Your files are never transmitted over the internet to our servers
  • Your files are never stored anywhere other than your own device's temporary browser memory
  • We have no ability to view, access, or retain your files
  • When you close the browser tab, no trace of your file remains accessible to anyone

This applies to all file types including PDF, DOCX, JPG, PNG, Excel, and PowerPoint files.

3 Data We Do Collect

Anonymous Usage Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics tools to understand how our Service is used in aggregate. This data is anonymous and may include:

  • Pages visited and approximate time spent
  • Which tools are used (tool name only, not file content)
  • General geographic region (country level only)
  • Browser type and device category (desktop/mobile)
  • Referrer URL (the site that linked to us)

This data is never linked to your identity and cannot be used to identify you personally.

Contact Form Submissions

If you contact us via the contact form or email, we receive and store:

  • Your name and email address
  • The content of your message

This information is used solely to respond to your enquiry. We do not add you to marketing lists without explicit consent, and we delete correspondence after it is resolved.

Server Logs

Our hosting provider may automatically collect standard web server logs including IP addresses and request timestamps for security and infrastructure purposes. These logs are retained for a short period and are not used for profiling.

4 Cookies

We use a minimal number of cookies:

  • Essential cookies: Required for the website to function. These do not track you across sites.
  • Analytics cookies: Anonymous usage data as described above. You can opt out via your browser settings or a browser extension like uBlock Origin.
  • Advertising cookies: Set by third-party ad networks if ads are displayed (see Section 5). You can manage these via your browser or a consent manager.

You can disable cookies entirely in your browser settings, though this may affect some site functionality.

5 Advertising

DocConverter is free to use. To sustain the project, we may display advertisements from third-party advertising networks such as Google AdSense.

⚠️ Advertisers operate independently and may set their own cookies and track your browsing behaviour across websites according to their own privacy policies. This is outside our control.

We do not share your personal data with advertisers. Ads are served based on general page context and/or the advertiser's own cookie-based profiling. You can opt out of personalised advertising via:

  • Google's Ad Settings: adssettings.google.com
  • Your browser's privacy settings or an ad blocker
  • The Network Advertising Initiative opt-out at optout.networkadvertising.org

6 Third-Party Services

DocConverter loads conversion libraries from the jsDelivr CDN. When these scripts load, jsDelivr may log your IP address and browser type as a standard part of CDN operation. See jsDelivr's Privacy Policy for details.

Google Fonts are loaded to display our site typography. Google may collect data when fonts are fetched. See Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any other third parties.

7 Children's Privacy

DocConverter is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it promptly.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has submitted personal data to us, please contact us at privacy@docconverter.app.

8 Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights under applicable privacy law (including GDPR, CCPA, and similar regulations). Because we collect minimal personal data, most of these rights are straightforward to exercise:

  • Right of access: You may request what personal data we hold about you. For most users, this is nothing (since we don't collect personal data from tool usage).
  • Right to erasure: If you contacted us, we can delete your correspondence upon request.
  • Right to portability: We can provide any personal data we hold in a standard format.
  • Right to opt out: You can opt out of analytics via browser settings or ad blocker at any time.
  • Right to complain: You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@docconverter.app.

9 Security

Because your files are processed locally in your browser and never transmitted to us, the primary security risk is on your own device. We recommend:

  • Using DocConverter on a trusted, private device
  • Keeping your browser and operating system up to date
  • Not using DocConverter on public shared computers for sensitive documents

For the limited personal data we do hold (contact form submissions), we take appropriate technical measures to protect it from unauthorised access, alteration, or destruction.

10 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Material changes will be noted on our homepage for a reasonable period.

Your continued use of DocConverter after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
